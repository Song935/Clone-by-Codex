# Community conduct

- Be respectful and specific in technical discussions. Critique the change, not the contributor.
- Welcome beginners, different backgrounds and different languages.
- Do not harass, threaten, disclose personal information, or share private drawings or credentials.
- Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and restrict participation when necessary.

For conduct or security issues, request a private contact route from the maintainer without posting sensitive details in a public issue.
